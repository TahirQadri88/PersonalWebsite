Aslam

A bold Naskh-inspired Urdu display font, copyright Axis SoftMedia Pvt.
Ltd. (Mumbai, 2008), designed by Syed Manzar Hasan Zaidi. Widely
distributed as a free download, but no open-source or redistribution
license was found for it — this is not an SIL OFL / CC-licensed font
the way Mehr Nastaliq Web (also in this folder) is.

Used here anyway, at the author's explicit direction, accepting the
redistribution risk that carries.

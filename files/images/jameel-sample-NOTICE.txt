The words جمیل نوری نستعلیق set in Jameel Noori Nastaleeq, 1,231 bytes,
316x158 at 2x, 32 colours, on a transparent ground.

Why a picture and not type: the reading-face panel lets a reader choose
by looking at the two faces rather than by reading a font's name. But
drawing a live Jameel sample for somebody who does not have Jameel would
start the 6.57MB download from the act of opening the panel — spending
their data before they asked, which is the exact thing the control
exists to let them decline. So when Jameel is off, its sample is this
file; when Jameel is already on, the panel draws live type and never
fetches this.

Quantised from a 9,008-byte capture. 32 colours costs a mean shift of
about 0.12/255 and saves most of the file; 16 colours saves a few
hundred bytes more at ten times the shift, which is not a trade worth
making on type.

Transparent, not on the page's cream, and that was found by LOOKING at
the panel rather than by measuring it: a cream rectangle reads as a box
drawn around the one row that has a picture, and it would fight the
warm ground that marks the row a reader has chosen. It is also smaller
— 1,231 bytes against 2,685 — because the ground was most of the
pixels.
